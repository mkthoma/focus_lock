Session Modes
Drift Signals
Border pulse
Pulsing orange border 0–60s on a drift domain
Toast notification
Non-blocking reminder after 60 seconds
Drift overlay
Full-page overlay with session stats after 3 minutes
System notifications
OS-level alert on session end and drift (requires permission)
Drift Warning Timers
Tier 1 — Red page flash + toast notification (seconds on a blocked site)
Tier 2 — Full-page overlay popup (seconds on a blocked site)
Tier 1 must be less than Tier 2. The red flash uses a slow 4-second pulse (0.25 Hz) and is safe for photosensitive users.
